CityCare Hospital Policy Document

1. Hospital Overview
CityCare Hospital is a multi-specialty healthcare institution dedicated to providing quality medical services to patients. The hospital operates 24 hours a day and offers emergency, outpatient, inpatient, diagnostic, and surgical services.

2. Patient Admission Policy
Patients must provide valid identification and complete the registration process before admission.
Emergency patients will receive immediate medical attention.
Admission records must be maintained accurately.

3. Visiting Hours Policy
General visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
Visitors must obtain a visitor pass before entering patient wards.
Children under the age of 12 are not allowed in intensive care units (ICU).

4. Medical Records Policy
Patient records are confidential and accessible only to authorized personnel.
Medical records must be stored securely.
Patient information cannot be shared without proper consent except when required by law.

5. Emergency Care Policy
Emergency services are available 24/7.
Critical patients receive treatment priority under the emergency triage system.
Emergency response teams must be available at all times.

6. Staff Conduct Policy
Hospital staff must maintain professional behavior.
All employees must wear identification badges while on duty.
Patient privacy and dignity must be respected at all times.

7. Infection Control Policy
Healthcare workers must follow hand hygiene protocols.
Protective equipment such as gloves and masks should be used when required.
Isolation procedures must be followed for infectious diseases.

8. Medicine and Pharmacy Policy
Medications should only be dispensed by licensed pharmacists.
Expired medicines must be removed from inventory immediately.
Prescription records must be maintained accurately.

9. Billing and Payment Policy
Patients are responsible for paying hospital charges before discharge unless covered by insurance.
Detailed billing statements will be provided upon request.
Accepted payment methods include cash, debit cards, credit cards, and digital payments.

10. Discharge Policy
Patients may be discharged only after approval from the attending physician.
Discharge summaries must be provided to patients.
Patients should receive instructions regarding medications and follow-up appointments.

11. Complaint Resolution Policy
Patients can submit complaints through the hospital help desk.
All complaints must be reviewed within 7 working days.
Hospital management aims to resolve issues fairly and promptly.
